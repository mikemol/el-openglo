# el-openglo design log — mechanized

*The log is a claim-DAG; this is what it says when read structurally.*

## Reading the log structurally

The design log parses structurally — its sessions, symbols, closures and gates are read by a tool rather than by eye [@PARSE]; the symbol ledger is read as the series it is, taking the last block as current rather than any earlier snapshot [@LEDGER].

## Where the log contradicts itself

Every contradiction, ungated closure and unresolved symbol in the log is either fixed or waived with a stated reason [@COHERENCE].

## The open worklist, by operator

The open worklist is bucketed by who can act on it, so work only a human at a real desktop can verify is never mistaken for work a check could close [@OPERATOR] (grounded above in [@LEDGER]).
